Possession & Handover

Understand how property possession works — from lease end and inspection to escrow release and taking the keys.

Home Possession

Signed Agreement

The lease or sale agreement is signed digitally. Key dates and responsibilities are recorded.

Inspection

Property is inspected against the condition report. Any issues are logged before handover.

Keys & Possession

The tenant or buyer receives the keys and officially takes possession from the handover date.

Deposit / Escrow

The security deposit or sale amount is held in escrow and released only after a clean handover.

The Handover Process

Five clear milestones — from signed agreement to taking the keys.

1

Finalize the agreement

Complete and sign the lease or sale agreement, confirming the intended possession date.

2

Conduct inspection

Perform the final walkthrough and update the condition report together.

3

Resolve utility dues

Settle any pending bills and record meter readings on the handover date.

4

Release escrow

Once handover is confirmed, escrow releases the deposit or payment to the rightful party.

5

Take possession

Keys are handed over and possession begins as documented.
