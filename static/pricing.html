Pricing FAQ

Pricing Questions

What does the Tenant Access Pass include?

Depending on your plan, you get access to view owner contact details, chat directly with owners, and book home tours for 3, 6, or 12 months. Choose ₹299/3mo, ₹459/6mo, or ₹799/12mo.

What's the difference between Owner plans?

All Owner Plus plans include boosted visibility and the Verified badge. 6-month adds priority tenant matching and faster payouts. 12-month adds featured listing boosts and unlimited leases.

When do owners have to pay?

Listing is always free. Owner Plus plans are optional upgrades for faster renting. You only pay if you choose to upgrade.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Our pricing is completely transparent. What you see is what you pay. No hidden costs, no broker commissions.
