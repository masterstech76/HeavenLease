01 · Repair control

Stay on Top of Repairs

A well-maintained property attracts better tenants, commands higher rent, and avoids expensive emergency repairs. Maintenance management is about having a simple, repeatable system for every repair — big or small.

HeavenLease is building a maintenance tracker so you can log issues, assign vendors and monitor costs from one dashboard.

02 · Preventive care

Preventive Maintenance Schedule

Preventive checks cost a fraction of emergency repairs. Waterproofing, electrical panel inspection, AC servicing and geyser flushing should be on a calendar, not done reactively.

A simple seasonal checklist — before summer, monsoon and winter — keeps your property in its best condition all year.

03 · Vendor network

Working With Vendors

Keep 2–3 trusted vendors per trade. Get written quotes for bigger jobs, agree on scope before work starts, and photograph before/after. Tenants appreciate fast, well-documented fixes — and it shows you care.
