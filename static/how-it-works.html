FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is HeavenLease really free for tenants?

Yes! Searching, messaging, and booking tours are completely free for tenants. We only charge owners a small fee when a lease is successfully signed.

How are properties verified?

Every owner must upload ownership proof and government ID, and our admin team reviews each owner application before the owner is marked verified.

What is escrow protection?

Your security deposit is held in a secure escrow account and only released to the owner after you move in and verify the property condition matches the listing.

How fast can I find a home?

Most tenants find their perfect home within 3-7 days. Our comfort-first search helps you narrow down options quickly.
