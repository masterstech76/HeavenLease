Home Comfort Scores
How We Score

Every Home, Rated On What Matters

Each score is 0–100, built from verified data at the property.

Quietness

Noise level from the street, neighbours, and common areas. Higher is calmer — perfect for focused work and restful sleep.

0 = busy road · 100 = silent

Sunlight

Natural light and ventilation through the day. Sunlit homes feel bigger, brighter, and healthier.

0 = no direct light · 100 = full daylight

Commute

Proximity to metro stations, bus stops, offices, and universities. Higher = closer to daily destinations.

0 = far from everything · 100 = walkable

Pet-Friendly

Properties that welcome pets are flagged and ranked with your furry family in mind. No surprises on move-in day.

Furnishing

Fully furnished, semi-furnished, and empty options — so you only pay for what you actually need.

Verified Owners

Every owner is document-verified and every listing is checked, so the score you see is the home you get.
