Ready-to-Use Lease Agreements

A well-written lease protects both you and your tenant. HeavenLease provides lease templates aligned with the common rental practices in your state, so you don't have to draft anything from scratch.

Templates cover the essentials every rental agreement needs — from rent and deposit to maintenance and termination.

Registering Your Agreement

In many Indian cities, lease agreements longer than 11 months should be registered and stamped. We explain exactly what registration involves, what it costs, and who typically pays, so there are no surprises later.

An 11-month template (renewable) is often the practical choice for both parties — it keeps things flexible while still being formal.

Customisation Tips

Never copy a template blindly. Adjust it for your specific property — pet policy, parking, society rules, or equipment included. Both parties should read the final agreement together before signing, and each should keep a copy.
