The approach

Background checks, made clear.

The process is designed around safety, privacy and practical rental decisions—so owners know what they are reviewing and applicants know what to expect.

What is checked?

A background check can review relevant public records and information that may help an owner assess rental risk and safety.

What stays private?

Screening is not intended to investigate protected characteristics or private areas of a tenant’s life unrelated to rental risk.

How long does it take?

Many checks resolve quickly, while timing can vary with jurisdiction and record availability. You are notified when the report is ready.

Fairness matters

Review the whole picture.

A record by itself should not be treated as an automatic rejection. HeavenLease encourages informed, context-aware decisions.

Past records are not automatic disqualifiersConsider the nature, age and relevance of a finding before making a rental decision.
Protected characteristics are off-limitsScreening should remain focused on legitimate rental safety and risk considerations.
Reports should be factualUse available information as context rather than assumptions about an applicant.
Keep the process transparentApplicants can follow their verification status and understand what happens next.
