Identity Verification

Every tenant is verified against government-issued ID before they can contact you or book a tour.

Home Tenant Screening · Identity Verification

What is Identity Verification?

Identity verification confirms that a prospective tenant is exactly who they claim to be before any rental agreement is signed. On HeavenLease, every tenant who wants to contact you or book a tour has already submitted a government-issued ID, and we check that document against secure public and private records.

This is the first and most important line of defence. Rental fraud almost always starts with a fake or borrowed identity, so we verify every applicant up front rather than after a problem appears.

What Documents Are Accepted?

We accept the standard government-issued identity documents used across India. At least one primary document is required, and we prefer a second supporting document to strengthen the match.

All documents are encrypted and stored securely. We never display your tenant's full ID to you — you receive a verification summary with a pass/fail result and the details that matter for the lease.

How Long Does It Take?

Most identity checks complete within minutes. In rare cases where records are slow to respond or a document is unclear, verification can take up to 24 hours.

You will be notified the moment a tenant's identity check completes, so you never have to wait around guessing.

Why It Matters to You as an Owner

Renting to an unverified person can lead to unpaid rent, sub-letting without permission, or legal disputes that are expensive to resolve. Identity verification dramatically reduces this risk and gives you a documented record you can rely on.

It also protects genuine tenants — honest applicants benefit because owners are far more willing to rent to someone whose identity is already verified.
