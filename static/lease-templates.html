Registering Your Agreement

In many Indian cities, lease agreements longer than 11 months should be registered and stamped. HeavenLease explains what registration involves, what it costs, and who typically pays.

An 11-month template (renewable) is often the practical choice for both parties — it keeps things flexible while still being formal.

Important: Registration and stamping requirements can vary by location and agreement. Check the applicable local requirements before signing.

Never copy a template blindly. Adjust the agreement for your specific property and the actual arrangement between both parties.
