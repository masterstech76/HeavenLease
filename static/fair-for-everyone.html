How It Works

Fairness is engineered, not promised

A fair rental market needs honest prices, equal access to information, and a neutral referee when things go wrong. That is why every listing shows its true comparable rent, and why both sides see the same listing, inspection and escrow data.

Disputes about deposits, repairs or move-out are reviewed by our team against documented evidence, with both parties hearing the same outcome. Fairness here is not a slogan; it is a process you can see.
