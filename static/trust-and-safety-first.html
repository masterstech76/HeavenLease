Trust Is Built, Not Assumed

Safe renting starts with real verification. Every owner on HeavenLease is document-verified before their listing goes live — we check ownership proof and identity so you never deal with a fake landlord.

Tenants are verified too, through our screening checks. Both sides start from a position of trust, backed by evidence.

  • Document-verified owners with ownership proof
  • Government ID verification for tenants
  • Escrow-protected security deposits
  • Secure, encrypted communication

Your Deposit, Protected

Security deposits are held in escrow until move-out, so neither side can be taken advantage of. If the tenancy ends cleanly, the deposit returns automatically — no excuses, no delays.

Our escrow policy is transparent and detailed, and both sides know exactly how disputes are handled before they sign.
