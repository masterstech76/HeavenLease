Pricing Your Rental Right

Setting the right rent is a balance. Price too high and your property sits vacant; price too low and you leave money on the table every single month. HeavenLease helps you find the sweet spot with market data for your exact area.

This guide walks you through the factors that decide how much tenants will pay — and how to tune your price over time.

A practical benchmark

How to Compare Your Rent

Look at 15–20 comparable listings within a 2–3 km radius: similar BHK, furnishing and amenities. The average of the middle 80% gives a realistic market rate. Then add or subtract for your property's unique advantages.

On HeavenLease you can browse live listings in your area for free to build this comparison in minutes.

When to raise the rent: Most owners review rent once a year. Raise it only with fair notice (and within what your lease allows). The best time to raise is near renewal, when a tenant who likes the home will usually accept a modest increase rather than move.
