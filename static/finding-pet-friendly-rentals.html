Finding Pet-Friendly Rentals

Jul 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Finding a home that welcomes your dog or cat is one of the hardest parts of renting in India — but it is far from impossible. Here is how to find (and win) pet-friendly rentals without paying a “pet deposit” everywhere you turn.

1. Filter for pet-friendliness early

Almost half of owners will bend the rules for the right tenant. But you save everyone time by searching where pets are welcomed from the start. Use our pet-friendly Comfort Score tag while browsing, so you only shortlist homes that are likely to say yes.

2. Build a pet resume

Owners worry about damage, noise, and neighbours. A short “pet resume” answers those fears before they arise:

  • Breed, age, weight, and a vet’s health certificate.
  • Proof of vaccination and deworming — owners love this.
  • A line about training: toilet habits, and that they are crate-trained or daytime-housebroken.
  • References from your previous landlord saying your pet caused no damage.

3. Meet in the middle on the hard asks

  • Pet clause: offer a clear agreement that you’ll cover any verified pet damage, just like normal wear.
  • Cleaning: volunteer for a professional deep-clean on move-out — it costs little and reassures a lot.
  • Neighbours: suggest a quick informal chat so the owner sees your pet is calm and quiet.
  • Additional deposit: agree to a modest, refundable pet deposit only against written “no pet damage” terms — never a non-refundable fee without a strong reason.

4. Know what to look for in the flat itself

A ground floor or one with a balcony and lift access beats an open staircase for older pets. Check window grilles and balcony railings for safety, and confirm the society’s own pet rules — some societies ban pets even when the owner is willing, so ask up front.

5. Be an ideal tenant in every other way

The pet is only one variable. Verified identity, a stable job, on-time payments, and a clean rental history make owners comfortable taking the “small risk” of a pet. The more solid your profile, the more negotiable the pet policy becomes.

Final word: owners rent to people they trust, and pets are just part of the package when that trust exists. Filter well, present yourself well, and you’ll find a home where both you and your furry friend are welcome.
