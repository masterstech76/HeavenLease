Comfort is measurable

Rental platforms usually show you four walls and a rent figure. We believe the daily experience - how much light you get, how noisy the street is, how long your commute actually takes, whether your pet is welcome - decides whether a house becomes a home.

Our Comfort Scores quantify exactly those things for every listing: sunlight, quietness, commute and pet-friendliness. You compare homes on the dimensions that affect your happiness, not just on square feet and rent.
