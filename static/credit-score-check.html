Soft credit check

A consent-based check designed to review credit history without the impact associated with a hard loan inquiry.

Clear signals

Understand repayment behaviour, defaults, existing debt and active credit accounts through a simplified result.

Privacy first

Owners receive a practical rating rather than the tenant's raw credit file, helping protect sensitive financial details.

What the check reveals

The credit report summarises past borrowing and repayment behaviour across loans, credit cards and other credit facilities.

  • Repayment and missed-payment history
  • Defaults and unusual recent activity
  • Existing debt and active accounts

How results are used

HeavenLease presents a simple result to help owners make a faster, more consistent decision.

  • Strong
  • Moderate
  • Needs Review

Consent & privacy

Credit checks are only run with the applicant's written consent and are used solely to assess rental suitability.

The information is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Your choice

An applicant can choose to rent from an owner who does not require a credit check. Screening is intended to provide additional assurance, not replace fair rental decisions.
