Our Mission

Renting Should Be Simple

Finding a rental home shouldn't require paying a broker a month's rent, dealing with hidden property flaws, or navigating endless phone calls. We believe everyone deserves a home that matches their comfort — not just their budget.

HeavenLease connects tenants directly with verified property owners. Our comfort-first search lets you filter by quietness, sunlight, commute time, and pet policy — so you find a home that truly fits your lifestyle.

For owners, we provide verified tenants, automated rent collection, and digital lease management — all in one dashboard. No more chasing payments or dealing with unreliable tenants.

Direct. Trusted. Comfortable.

Every owner is document-checked before they become verified, and tenants can pay security deposits into escrow-protected payments. We've built the platform we wished existed when we were renting.

Our Values

What We Stand For

No Brokers, Ever

We eliminate the middleman entirely. Tenants connect directly with owners, saving up to a month's rent in broker fees.

Trust & Safety First

Every owner is document-verified before they are marked verified, and tenants can pay security deposits into escrow-protected payments.

Comfort Over Everything

We score every property on quietness, sunlight, commute, and pet-friendliness — because comfort matters as much as cost.

Speed & Simplicity

From search to signed lease in days, not weeks. Our digital-first approach makes renting faster than ever.

Fair for Everyone

Transparent pricing, fair lease terms, and balanced protections for both tenants and owners.

Community Focused

We partner with local services — movers, Wi-Fi providers, and cleaners — to make moving easier for everyone.
