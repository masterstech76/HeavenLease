Why Employment Verification?

Employment verification confirms that a tenant has a stable source of income and can genuinely afford the rent. It checks the employer's details, the tenant's role, and their income against what they told you.

This is especially useful for working professionals — a steady job and dependable pay can provide additional confidence when evaluating an application.

How We Verify

We verify employment through official channels where possible — company email domains, HR contact, payslips, or bank statement checks for self-employed applicants. Every check is done with the tenant's consent, and the applicant is told exactly what is being verified and why.

Rent-to-Income Ratio

As a rule of thumb, many owners prefer rent to be no more than 40–50% of monthly take-home income. We compute this ratio and highlight it on the applicant's profile, but the final decision is always yours.

Use the ratio as a screening signal, not an automatic rejection rule.
Consider income stability alongside the tenant's overall application.

Self-Employed Tenants

Self-employed applicants can be verified through GST registration, business bank statements, formal registrations or audited income. Being self-employed is not a negative — it simply requires a different kind of proof.
